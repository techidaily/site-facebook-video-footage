1\. Fully **articulated screen** (flip screen)

2\. Good **continual autofocus** while recording video

4\. Touch **to focus**

5\. Good **low-light performance**

6\. **external mic jack**, **hot/cold shoe** bracket

7\. **optical image stabilization**
